# Battle mode (game mode 1), 2026-11-02

## Where it is offered

`menu.WGM` sets game mode 0 or 1 through `WMenuSetValueItem` type 0 on the two-player page 47 (#48 value 0, #54 value 1, text 24). The one-player page 19 offers only 0, 3 and 2. A battle is therefore a two-player game.

The course file is `<env>/<env>_b.wgm` (`0x486f90`'s battle table, `game_files`).

## Arenas

- `cc_b`, `ct_b`, `dt_b`, `sh_b` and `dr_b` each have three WMOD routes.
- On `ct_b`, `sh_b` and `dr_b`, two of them have no ground brick (ground id 0).
- There is no WTrackCamera. Only `sh_b` has a WStartLine.
- Route 0's first row links two WPortals, `+0x48` and `+0x50`. On `cc_b` these are 349 and 348.

## Placement (`0x49eb70`, mode 1, `0x49fa55`)

- **The player (`+0x38`):** the frame of the row's `+0x48` object, moved 300 (`0x512610`) along its z axis.
- **The opponent:** `+0x54` (player 2) when `app+0x1a64` is set, else the robot `+0x60`.
  - It takes the `+0x50` object's frame with its x and z axes multiplied by −1 (`0x5121e4`), moved −300 (`0x514bb8`) along the original z.
- Both get `+0x3a3` = 0. The row is the manager's `+0x8d0` for the route `+0x8c4`. Native uses route 0, as the original (below, 2026-11-11).

## The count

- `+0x3a3` is incremented by WackyAutoBomba's activation (`0x47d756`), on the car that is hit. So it counts the hits **received**.
- The limit `app+0x1a74` is 5 in mode 1 (`0x4a0602`; `0x40c4f7` copies a menu value for the other modes).
- **State 9 (`0x4a3136`, table `0x4a354c` index 8):** when the player's or the opponent's count reaches the limit:
  - both cars get `+0x2b1` = `0x10000000` (brake to a stop);
  - the state becomes 10;
  - with two players the counter `+0xf34` = 60 (`0x4a31ef`); with one, it is not set.
- **State 10 (`0x4a31fe`):** the counter runs down, then a new Return (DIK `0x1c`) leaves.

## HUD

- **`0x485b00`, mode 1:** children 3–12 are hidden. Child 13 shows `%d` of the block's `+0x20`, child 14 that of `+0x24`.
- **`0x4aa68c`** copies the counts into the blocks:
  - player 1's own count to `0x6f3168` and the opponent's to `0x6f316c`;
  - in two players, player 2's own count to `0x6f31bc` and player 1's to `0x6f31c0`.
- **Positions:** (14, 216) and (306, 216) in one player; (14, 105) and (306, 105) in each half. Child 14 is right-aligned (`0x4b` = `0xa0000`).
- **The strip** (`0x499c38`) draws only in states 1–3, so not in a battle.
- **End screen (`0x4aa440`, per viewport `+0x13a`):**
  - with this player's count at the limit, string 6 TU AS PERDU!; otherwise string 5 TU AS GAGNE!, in a pulsing colour (sin of the time). Either is drawn at (160, 60) over a black copy at (162, 62);
  - then string 145 APPUYER SUR ENTREE POUR CONTINUER at (160, 100);
  - then the literal "CONTINUE - X" (`0x52d210`) at (160, 200).

## Native

- **`RaceTrack(course, scene, battle)`:** the caller passes battle for a `*_b.wgm`.
  - `battle_frame(0/1)` gives the two spawns; `start_line()` is the player's.
  - The loop transform is optional, and routes without ground make no section.
  - `load_world` accepts a scene without a camera (identity view).
- **`RaceSession`, mode 1:**
  - the cars start at the spawns;
  - no laps are counted;
  - `hit()` counts `frags`;
  - the end is state 10, with counter 60 in two players and 0 in one; both cars brake to a stop.
- **`RaceView`:** `hud`/`hud2` draw the counts and the end screen and hide the strip, laps, rank and timer.
- **`linux_main`:** an arena runs as a battle.

## Checks

`native_drive_test` on the five arenas checks:
- the spawns;
- that an arena refuses a race mode;
- five bomb hits on the robot ending the battle, with the player winning and no laps;
- Enter accepted at once in one player;
- player 2 at the second spawn, and the 60-update wait, in two players.

An Xvfb capture of `dt_b` shows the two counts at the bottom corners.

## The winner's colour (2026-11-11)

`0x4aa480..0x4aa4c5`: a colour (255, v, v) with v = sin(timeGetTime × 0.01 (`0x512fcc`)) × 63
(`0x514be0`) + 192 (`0x514bd0`), the standings' pulse. String 5 TU AS GAGNE! is set with it
(`0x4aa534`, the colour at `[esp+0xc]`); string 6 TU AS PERDU! gets no colour (`push 0`, `0x4aa4f1`),
so the default. The shadow at (162, 62) is black. Native: `RaceView::battle_pulse`, in `hud` and
`hud2`. Built and ctest only; no capture of a battle's end.

## The route `+0x8c4` (2026-11-11)

The race setup `0x49dc90` zeroes `esi` (`0x49dcad`) and stores it in the manager's `+0x8c4` in each of
its three start branches (`0x49dd44`, `0x49ded9`, `0x49e0eb`); these are the only stores to
`+0x8c4` in WPC.EXE. The battle placement (`0x49fa55`) then copies it into the player's `+0x1b7` and
uses it as the scene group (`+0x104`) and the row index. So a battle always starts from route 0's
first row, as the port does (`RaceTrack`); nothing to change.

## Against Wine (2026-11-12)

Both builds from the menu with the same keys: DEMARRER, 2 JOUEURS, MODE AFFRONTEMENT, Return on the
car page (both keyboard players ready at once, see `analysis/two-player-20261101.md`), Le Grand Canyon,
`cc_b`.

- **Radar (ported 2026-11-12).** Wine draws an orange disc with crosshair rings at each half's top
  left, with a green dot for the opponent.
  - `0x4aa120` computes the dot: the opponent (`+0x54` with two players, else the robot `+0x60`) in
    the player's frame (`0x43c930`, `0x43b880`), × manager `+0xf90` = record `+0x7c` / `+0x84`
    (`0x49e5af`), clamped to `+0x7c` (`0x4aa1d1..0x4aa1e5`, normalised by `0x452e20`), then
    `+0xf94` = ftol(x + 40), `+0xf9c` = ftol(40 − z) (`0x512dd8` = 40) for player 1, and
    `+0xf98`/`+0xfa0` around 32 (`0x5127b4`) for player 2's view of player 1.
  - The manager film's draw `0x499b30`, battle branch `0x499b96`: frame 1 (`+0x162` = 1) at the dot
    (`+0x1aa`, `+0x1b2`), then frame 0 at (40, 40) in viewport 0 or (32, 32) in viewport 1
    (`0x499bd9`, `0x499c0f`). So the radar is the same film whose frames vehicle + 2 are the strip's
    heads: frame 0 the disc, frame 1 the dot.
  - Wine memory reads (manager by vtable `0x514b70`): `+0xf90` = 0.002, the record's
    `+0x7c` = 20 and `+0x84` = 10000 (so `m_nRadarRadius` 20 and `m_nRadarRange` 10000 at run time),
    dots (58, 33) and (25, 13); `m_idRadar`/`m_idEnemyMark` (`+0xf80..+0xf8c`) are null and unused.
  - Screen (1024×768): the discs' centres at (130, 128) and (102, 100) pixels from each half's top
    left, i.e. the 320-unit positions at the window's uniform scale 3.2.
  - Native: `RaceView::radar` (in `hud2` and the one-player battle `hud`), frames at scale 1 (0.65,
    the heads' scale, made the disc 0.65 of Wine's). After: both discs and dots within 2 px of
    Wine's.
- **Counts.** Wine shows one count per half, at the right (306, 105); the port drew both (14, 105)
  and (306, 105). Traced: in mode 1 (`0x4860d0`) child 13 (the block's `+0x20`, this player's own
  count) is shown only when `0x6f3140` = 1 (one player, `0x486105..0x486158`); child 14 (`+0x24`, the
  opponent's hits received) always (`0x48615e..0x4861a4`). `RaceView::hud2` now draws child 14 only;
  `hud` (one player) keeps both.
- **The top camera.** Wine's top half showed more sky, the port's the ground almost to the top.
  Wine memory reads (2026-11-12, cameras by the GDCamera vtable `0x5108e0`, cars by `0x51295c`,
  translations at object `+0x2c` + 40): player 1's camera 800 back and 100 up from its car, level;
  player 2's 900 back and 350 up. With two players `0x477ea0` writes view 0 = (0, 100, −800) and
  view 1 (`_2`) = (0, 350, −900), and the camera starts in view 1. After driving, player 1's stays 800
  and 100; one Return (player 1's CAMERA key, DIK `0x1c`) puts it at 900 and 350. So the Return that
  starts the battle (the environment page's accept; a battle has no countdown) reaches player 1's
  camera as a view press. The port's race starts after its load, with the key up; `linux_main` now
  gives a menu-started two-player battle's first update a view press for player 1 (provisional
  mechanism: the original's input word is not re-read between the menu and the race's first
  update). After: the top half frames like Wine's (same car size and horizon).
- The race rule for the end (state 10), the spawns and the HUD's text positions were not compared.
