# Hit smoke, the film blend, the hit tint, the stars and the paint pool (2026-11-07/08)

Native:
- `CarEffects::hit_smoke` and `CarEffects::update` (`race_effects.cpp`);
- `update_bomb`'s `BombStep::smoke`/`flip` (`race_weapons.cpp`) and `RaceSession::bomb_step`;

- `WPC_DRAW_INVERSE` (`linux_platform.cpp`).

## The smoke clone (WackyAutoBomba `0x47ed6e`)

- **Which hits:** types 8, 0x40 and 0x80 (the jump table `0x47f220`/`0x47f20c`, and `0x47ebd4` for
  0x80).
- **Counter:** each update of the hit, `+0x28` is incremented. The activation `0x47d450` resets it
  (`0x47d753`).
- **Spawn:** when `+0x28 % 5 == 0`, the action resolves its template record's `+0x44` through
  `0x433070`. That record holds `m_smokeparticle`, property `0x153`: `general.wgm` node `0x1be`.
  - It clones the template (`0x41fd00`, CreateHierarchy).
  - The clone takes the car's local matrix (`+0x2c`) and the car's group (`+0x104`), and goes under
    `[0x6f2c40]` (`0x41b3c0`).
- **The clone's film:** its first child's first action (`+0x60` → `+0x94`) is activated, and its
  position `+0x30..+0x38` is zeroed.
  - When `+0x28 % 10 == 0`, its `+0x40` is negated (`0x47ee53`).
- **The template:** GDStdBrick `0x1be`, with GD3DFilm `0x1bf`.
  - FILM `0x102f6`: one reel of two 60 × 60 frames. `m_AnimType` 0, so the film update `0x430110`
    never steps it and frame 0 stays.
  - White (`m_ColR/G/B` 255). `m_RenderSetting` is `0x2180042`.
  - The film carries the action `flamesmoke`.

## flamesmoke (record `0x541cb8`, registered at `0x417fc0`, class id `0xff2455c1`)

- **The class:** instance size 0x48, vtable `0x5103d0`; init `+0x10` = `0x4181e0`, update
  `+0x8` = `0x418310`.
- **Init:**
  - `+0x24` = 0, `+0x28` = 30, `+0x2c` = 0 (the velocity); `+0x3c` = 0 (the age); `+0x44` = 0.1.
  - `+0x40` = rand × 2π (`0x430fc0` → `0x430f30`, the effects stream `0x430ef0`).
  - On the film: the current frame is centred (offset −w/2, −h/2), `+0x18a` = 0.75, and `+0x12a` gets
    `0x2000000`.
- **Update:**
  - The age is incremented, and t = 2 × age.
  - Past t = 10 (`0x510254`): `+0x28` × 0.99 (`0x510420`), `+0x24` + 0.2 (`0x51041c`), then the
    position moves by the velocity.
  - x = ftol(t), and the film takes:
    - the position (`+0x54`, in the clone's frame);
    - the scale `+0x152` = 2 × curve `0x5264c8`(x);
    - the angle `+0x14a` = `+0x40` ± curve `0x526528`(x) (+ when `+0x40` > 0, else −);
    - the alpha `+0x1ba` = ftol(255 × curve `0x526588`(x)).
  - Past t = 100 (`0x510414`), the clone gets flag 1 and the action is deactivated.
- **Curves (`0x4184c0`):** `{count, keys}`, where a key is (int x, float y). They are linear
  between keys and held at the ends. Keys at x = 0, 10, …, 100:

  | Curve | Values |
  |---|---|
  | scale `0x5264c8` | 0, .2, .3, .4, .5, .58, .64, .7, .75, .79, .83 |
  | turn `0x526528` | 0, .698, .663, 1.326, 1.885, 2.304, 2.793, 3.316, 3.595, 3.630, 3.770 |
  | alpha `0x526588` | 0, .5, .5, .4, .4, .3, .3, .2, .2, .1, 0 |

- **Reader of `+0x18a`:** the film draw does not read it, and its reader was not identified.

## The film draw (`0x42d090`, Direct3D path `0x6f5958` = 1; Wine reads 1)

- **Corners:** ftol(6 × `+0x152` × offset) (`0x42d313`), turned by `+0x14a`.
- **Vertex colour:** `+0x132/+0x13a/+0x142`, with the alpha `+0x1ba`. The software path alone
  replaces the alpha by the material's TRAN (`+0x60`) when SMOO (`+0x68`) is 0 (`0x42d4f8`).
- **Texture flags:** with the alpha below 255, the film sets `0x10` and `0x20` (`0x42d592..`).
- **Draw mode (`0x42d5c4`):** 8, or 9 + (SMOO ≠ 0) when the frame material's SILL (`+0x5c`, loader
  `0x43a80c`) is nonzero. The mode goes to `0x4b5550`.
- **`0x4b5550`:**
  - ALPHABLENDENABLE is on.
  - With texture flag 8: alpha op MODULATE when `0x20` is set, else SELECTARG1 (texture). Without
    flag 8: the alpha op is DISABLE.
  - Bit 0x10: no culling.
  - Bit 0x2: SRCBLEND INVSRCALPHA, DESTBLEND INVSRCCOLOR.
  - ZWRITEENABLE is off for every film.
  - After the draw: cull mode 3, blending off, SRCALPHA/INVSRCALPHA, depth write on.
- **The flush (`0x4b32af`):** it draws the queued films after turning blending off (`0x1b` = 0) and
  setting the stage-0 alpha op to DISABLE.
- **Hence:** frames with SILL and SMOO (the smoke `0x102f6`, the POUSSIERE film `0x102e0`) draw
  dst × (1 − src) + src × (1 − α). The dust `0x102e6` (SMOO 0) and WKBumm `0x102b4` (SILL 0) keep
  SRCALPHA/INVSRCALPHA.
- **Native:** every film frame draws without depth write, with `WPC_DRAW_INVERSE` when SILL and SMOO
  are set. The explosion sprite (not a film) is unchanged.

## The hit tint

`0x47d890` rewrites every vertex colour of the car model. Natively, textured batches with 24-bit
textures counted as translucent, and the tint skipped them (provisional), so a hit car kept its
colours. Now the tint applies to a copy of all the model's batches, drawn like the model.

  with `+0x28` = `+0x82` = 17 half a second later.
  - The native run used `WPC_HIT=0x80,150`. Recordings were made at 30 fps on both sides.

## The hit models cloned at activation (`0x47d450`, 2026-11-08)

The template record (`[action+0x18]`) holds one id per member:
`m_TankGunHit` `+0x24`, `m_PaintBallHit` `+0x2c`, `m_Particle` `+0x34`, `m_BoomerangHit` `+0x3c`,
`m_smokeparticle` `+0x44`, `m_bluepool` `+0x4c`. In `general.wgm` they are `0x392`, 1, `0x394`, `0x391`,
`0x1be` and `0x1c1`.

- **The activation:**
  - `+0x32` = car `+0x24f` (the body model), `+0x3e` its mesh, `+0x42` its vertex count.
  - Types 0x10/0x100 (`0x47d515`): `+0x4e` = a clone of `m_BoomerangHit`, under `[0x6f2c40]`.
  - Type 4: `+0x52` = a clone of `m_bluepool`, its basis reset to the identity (`0x47d609`).
  - Type 2: `+0x5a` = a clone of `m_TankGunHit`, whose child's vtable `+0x4c` is called;
    `+0x7e` = `m_Particle` resolved, not cloned.
- **The deactivation** (`0x47f690`) clears `+0x4e`, `+0x5a`, `+0x52` and `+0x56`.

### The stars (types 0x10/0x100, `0x47ebea`)

- **The model:** GDModel `0x391` (GMDL `0x102f0`, local matrix × 1.5, `m_RenderSetting` `0x80062`, a
  WPalAnim action). Natively it is drawn without the WPalAnim.
- **Every update** it gets flag `0x2` (shown), then by stage (`+0x3a`):
  - 0: the basis × 0.1 (`0x43b190`), and the car's group;
  - 1: × 1.3; once `+0x28` passes 8, the basis becomes the identity (`0x43b090`: all 13 dwords, with
    the diagonal 1) × 1.5.
- **Then:** the translation is the car's position plus 120 in y (`0x512bf4`), and the basis turns
  20 degrees about y (`0x3eb2b8c2`, `0x43b4a0`).
- **Hidden** (`0x2` cleared) once `+0x82` passes 45. The hit itself ends at 0x2e.
- **Native:** `BombHit::SpinModel` (stage, scale, from_identity, turn) and `RaceView::submit_spin_hit`.

### The paint pool (type 4, `0x47ea3b`, stage table `0x47f254`)

- **The model:** GDModel `0x1c1` (GMDL `0x102f5`, identity matrix).
- **Stages:**
  - 0: the frame of the car's body model `[+0x32]`, × 0.2, shown (flag `0x100` cleared), with that
    model's group;
  - 1: × (1.15, 1, 1.15) each update; past `+0x82` = 16, `+0x28` = 0;
  - 2: until `+0x28` passes 50;
  - 3: × (0.8, 1, 0.8) until `+0x28` passes 6;
  - 4: hidden (`0x100`).
- **The end:** `0x47d890(20, 20, 255, 10)` done at stage 5 ends the hit, at counter 78 (as the earlier
  native end rule had it).
- **Native:** `BombHit::PaintPool`. Stage 0 sets `BombStep::pool`, and `RaceSession::bomb_step` stores
  the car's frame. The view draws the pool in `initial_vehicle_model_frame(frame)`, with its axes
  scaled.

### Compared with Wine (`cc_t1`, the player, one-shot hooks for 0x10 and for 4)

- **0x10:** the ring of stars circles the car at the same height and size, grows, then turns at 1.5,
  and is gone after about 1.5 s. This matches the native capture.
- **4:** the car turns blue and a blue pool spreads under it, then shrinks. This matches the native
  capture.
- **Not aligned:** the update-by-update timing.

## Not ported

- **Dropped:** `+0x18a`, and the film's own frame-offset rewrite (natively the x offset already
  serves both axes).
- **The order of a clone's first update against the bomba** is not traced; natively it is the same
  update.
- **The tank gun's hit (type 2):** its model `+0x5a` (GDModel `0x392` with GDHModel `0x393`, an
  animation started through vtable `+0x4c`), `m_Particle` (`0x394`, a GDModel with `WReszecske`),
  and the hiding of the car and its wheels.
- **The stars' WPalAnim** is not drawn.

## The tank gun's hit, type 2 (2026-11-08)

### Recovered

- **The template.** WackyAutoBomba's `m_TankGunHit` (property `0x14f`, `+0x24`) is general.wgm
  GDModel `0x392`, a container with no GMDL. Its child GDHModel `0x393` has GHMS `0x102f1` (70
  vertices, 6 segments) and no MTNL. `m_Particle` (`0x151`) is GDModel `0x394`, with a
  `WReszecske` action.
- **The stages** (`0x47e277`, jump table `0x47f230`; `+0x28` counts within a stage, `+0x82` is the
  hit's counter):
  - **0** (`0x47e2c8`, counter 0): the clone's matrix takes the car's frame (position `0x4038f5`;
    axes `0x405065`, `0x401f64`, `0x4024fa`), times 2 (`0x402027`). It is shown (`+0xc` |= 2) and
    gets the car's group. The car, its wheels (`+0x273..+0x27f`) and `+0x32` get flag `0x100`
    (hidden).
  - **1** (`0x47e49b`): the same each update; at `+0x28` = 5 the next stage.
  - **2** (`0x47e60d`, counters 6..41): the position alone follows. With t = `+0x28` × 20 degrees
    (`0x51152c`, `0x511518`), the overrides `0x437f70` (flag 1) set:
    - segment 1 to (1, sin t × 0.5 + 1, 1);
    - segment 3 to (sin 4t × 0.5 + 1, 1, 1);
    - segment 4 to (sin 2t × 0.5 + 1, 1, 1);
    - segment 5 to (1, 1, sin 2t × 0.5 + 1).
    Past `+0x28` = 35 they go back to 1 with flag 0.
  - **3, 4 and 5 in one update** (counter 42; the code falls through `0x47e882` and `0x47e973`):
    - eleven `m_Particle` clones at the car's frame, their WReszecske at the car's position plus
      v = 30, 34 .. 70 in y;
    - the car's parts shown again;
    - its vertex colours (100, 100, 0) (`0x3ec8c8c9` on the Direct3D path);
    - the model's axes × (0.7, 0.1, 0.7).
  - **6** (`0x47e9a2`): × (1.1, 1, 1.1) an update; past `+0x28` = 5 the next stage.
  - **7** (`0x47e9de`): waits for the counter to pass 25.
  - **8** (`0x47e9f0`, counter 50): hidden; `0x404fde(100, 100, 0, 10)` fades the colours and ends
    the hit.
- **`0x437f70`** (segment, sx, sy, sz, flag): flag −1 clears the GDHModel's `+0x17a` bit 1.
  Otherwise it allocates the override pairs (`+0x176`, 9 per segment). With a flag it locks the
  scale slots 6..8: a = −1, b = s − 1, which `motion_value` resolves to b.
- **WReszecske** (vtable `0x512f98`, init `0x47d050`, update `0x47d130`; `rand(n)` = `0x430f80` =
  rand() % n):
  - **init:** v = ((r % 180 − 90) × 0.2, r % 90 × 0.2 + 20, (r % 180 − 90) × 0.2), all times
    (r % 60 × 0.01 + 0.7); `+0x40` = 0.
  - **update:** at `+0x40` = 0 the axes are scaled by r % 110 × 0.01 + 1 each. Past 30 the clone
    ends (`0x405a6a`). Otherwise:
    - v.y −= 1.5, p += v, and the translation is p;
    - the axes are scaled × (0.92, 0.92, 0.91);
    - the clone turns by 0.1 about x (`0x43b2b0`: y' = c y − s z, z' = s y + c z), then by 0.2
      about y (`0x43b4a0`).

## The tint's renderer path (2026-11-08)

The tint `0x47d890` runs its packed-byte path under Wine (`0x6f5958` = 1). The hits' fades take
33 updates, not 30, and stop short of the target until the next mode's write.
`tint_colour` (`race_weapons`) gives each vertex's colour for the car model and the swaying body.
See `analysis/weapons-bomba-20261029.md`.
