# Car effect states `+0x1c7` and dt_t1's trams (2026-10-30)

Native: `src/port/race_objects.{h,cpp}` (`CourseObjects`, loaded by
`RaceTrack`, copied and updated by `RaceSession::update_objects`), drawn by
`RaceView` (`crossing_trams_`, `movers_`).

On Championship I the only instance among these classes is dt_t1's WTram
(id `0x370`); the others appear only in the class registry every WGM carries.

**State 5 in WCtrlRoboCar:** far mode does nothing (`0x4e53f0`); near mode
brakes, throttle 2 above speed 0 and 1 below (`0x4e7db0..0x4e7e0b`), and the
slow counter `+0xa8` does not run (`0x4e6919`).

## WTram (brick, update `0x4caf40`, init `0x4cad90`)

- Members with a 4-byte base: `m_Pal` `+0x88` (8 bytes), `m_Vel` `+0x90`
  (dt_t1: 100), `m_Radius` `+0x98` (2000).
- Children: a `GDPath` (`+0x60`; two points, about 7610 apart) whose first
  child is the tram `GDModel` (`+0x123`, two PRIM spheres r 270), and the
  lights model (`+0x60` → `+0x14`).
- Init: `+0x16f` the path's points, `+0x163` the unit direction, `+0x15f`
  the length, `+0x15b` = 0, `+0x122` = `0xff`, `+0x11e` = 2.
- Light cycle `0x52d8cc`, {lamp A, lamp B, updates} with bits 1 red,
  2 amber, 4 green: {1,4,120} {1,2,30} {1,1,30} {4,1,120} {2,1,30} {1,1,30}.
  At timer 0 the phase advances (wrapping after 5) and the timer takes the
  phase's count; otherwise it counts down, so a phase lasts count + 1.
- Tram: while lamp B is green or `+0x15b` ≠ 0, `+0x15b` += m_Vel; at the
  length it returns to 0 and zeroes the timer (phase 0 is cut short:
  the cycle is 323 updates natively, 366 − 121 + 78).
- The tram's frame: the copied frame with its translation set to start +
  direction × `+0x15b` (`0x43bf90`), turned by π/2 (`0x43b2b0`), written to
  the tram. **Provisional:** the copy is taken as the tram's serialized
  frame and the turn as about its x axis; that stands the tram upright on
  its rails in the native capture (spheres at z −125 become 125 up).
- Hits: while `+0x15b` ≠ 0, the tram's spheres against the cars (mask
  `0x100`, `0x4a60c0`); an active car with an idle WackyAutoBomba gets type
  `0x200`. Natively every touching car is hit (the query returns one).
- Robots (`+0x18 → +0xc0` = 1) against the brick's position (`ecx = ebp` at
  `0x4cb22d`), `0x4c6f40` (integer box, then squared distance < m_Radius²):
  entering (flag `+499` bit 2 clear) with `+0x1c7` = 0 while A is red →
  5; inside with 5 while A is green → 0; the flag then follows.
- Enabling: the brick has `+0x104` = 2 (its section). The scene walker
  `0x432970` (recursive through `+0x60`/`+0x14`, thunk `0x4049cb`) enables
  an action of its `+0x94` list whose flag 2 is set and 0x1000 clear
  (`0x41ba60`: flags |= 0x1002, then vtable `+0x10`, the init), disables
  one with 0x1000 set and 2 clear (`0x41ba90`: vtable `+0x14`, flags &=
  ~0x1002) and updates (`+0x8`) those with 0x2002. WTram's `+0x14` is
  `0x4caed0`: it calls `0x404fe8` on the car manager, puts the tram's frame
  back from `+0x127` and calls `0x404124(0)` on the tram (hide,
  provisional). Flag 2 comes from the WMOD route rows (see "Section
  enabling" below).
- Lights: when `DAT_006f5958` = 1, the lamps' texture v is rewritten per
  phase (u bands 0.33/0.66, v < 0.24 lamp A, else B; lit 0, unlit 0.33).
  **Not ported.**

## WGoPath / WElTram (update `0x4d1cd0`, init `0x4d1a90`; WElTram end `0x4d2330`)

- WElTram (vtable `0x515ea4`) is WGoPath (`0x515e2c`) with `m_Waiting`
  (template `+0x6c`) and `m_Change`, and slot `+0x2c` = `0x4d2330`.
- Members: m_nStep (step 1 / m_nStep), m_nItemLength, m_nOffset (start
  `+0xc0` = offset, end `+0xc4` = 1 − length + offset), m_bLoop, m_bRotate,
  m_aZrotate (spin per update), m_bCollision, m_idPath, m_OnFinish.
- Update: with the end flag `+0xd0`, call `+0x2c` and return. Otherwise
  the car query (hit → `+0x30` = `0x4d1c80`: type `0x200`), the frame at t
  (`0x444be0`), the spin, then t += step; past the end with m_bLoop: t ∓ 1
  and the end flag; without it: clamp and deactivate.
- `0x444be0`: segments n − 1 (open) or n (closed); t × segments split into
  index and fraction; the point lerps, the tangent blends the segment's and
  the next one's (open ends repeat the neighbouring segment); at t = 1 on
  an open path the last point.
- With m_bRotate: `0x452f20` gives yaw atan2(−x, z) and pitch of the
  tangent and of the init segment `+0xb4`; the frame turns about y by the
  yaw difference − π/2 (`0x43b4a0`), about z by the spin (`0x43b690`), about
  x by the pitch difference − π/2 (`0x43b2b0`) — each post-multiplies.
- `0x4d2330`: counts updates; past m_Waiting the flag clears, t goes to
  the end it reached and the step changes sign (a shuttle).
- dt_t1: `GDModel 0x21d` (a tram of four parts, PRIM cylinder r 210 h 1620)
  on `GDPath 0x21f` (two points, 18896 apart along x, y 0), step 1/300,
  loop, rotate, waiting 0.

## Verification

- `native_drive_test` on dt_t1: one crossing, one mover; m_Vel 100, radius
  2000, path 7610; the green phase recurs every 323 updates; the tram
  moves 77 updates per pass; robots wait; the shuttle turns at both ends.
  Synthetic `path_point` checks (open, end, closed).
- Native captures (`WPC_RACE_PLACE=10500,22500,0` and `10000,13000,0`):
  the crossing tram leaves its tunnel upright along the rails; the shuttle
  runs along the square behind the park.
- With every robot kept near (test mode), a robot reaching the crossing at
  speed 0.8 under red brakes to x 7184, outside the 2000 zone, and keeps
  state 5 (it never sees green inside). The original's code reads the same
  way; whether its robots stop inside the zone was not checked.

## Wine comparison (2026-10-31)

Single race on `dt_t1` from a scratch prefix (disc extracted to the
scratchpad as `D:`). The `wmem.exe` probe found the brick by its vtable
`0x51585c` (update slot `+8`; vtable of the base `0x41e2f0` is
`0x510850`). A hook written into the running copy at `0x4cad90` and
`0x4caf40` (jump to a stub in `VirtualAllocEx` memory that counts calls and
stores the return addresses) gave the caller `0x432a5b`/`0x4329f0` inside
`0x432970`. The player was driven with XTest keys along a robot's logged
path into section 2 and parked at (12600, 41, 23790).

- **Enabling.** With the player in sections 0 and 1 (first lap) the brick
  was never initialised (its members held fill bytes) and no update ran.
  On a later lap the init ran on entering section 1 and the updates
  continued through sections 1, 2 and 3; they stopped in section 0. Five
  inits by the sixth pass: each enabling re-runs `0x4cad90`. Native:
  `TramCrossing::section` from the brick's route section, and the crossing
  runs while the player's section is it or a neighbour (sections as a
  loop), re-initialised on each enabling; a disabled tram is not drawn.
  The rule is now read from the code (below).
- **Lights.** Phase 0 recurred every 10.71 s, phase 3 lasted 4.0 s (120
  updates at 30 Hz): a cycle of ~321 updates against the native 323 (the
  probe sampled every 33 ms). `+0x15f` = 7610.26, as natively.
- **Robots** (six robots, 70 s, 50 ms samples): state 5 is set on entry at
  1733..1941 from the brick's position, the robots brake at ~0.025 per
  update (0.64 → 0 in ~12 updates; the dip before the crossing takes them
  from y −170 to −587) and stop at 796..1305 from it, inside the zone;
  state 0 returns when phase 3 (lamp A green) starts. Robots passing at
  green or amber never got 5. The native near robots brake at 0.023 per
  update. A native robot at 0.817 (always-near test pack, above the
  0.57..0.66 the original's robots had there) leaves the ground over the
  dip crest for ~15 updates, so `update_robot_speed` does not brake it,
  and stops at 2723, outside the zone; it keeps state 5 (the release needs
  the zone). Not seen under Wine.
- **Far robots, section 2 disabled.** With the player in sections 0/1 the
  original's far robots stood still for ~3.2 s at section 2's entry
  (16182, 17, 19987) and then appeared in section 3 at (1048, 38, 23084);
  native far robots drive through section 2 in ~200 updates. Not traced.
- Not seen: the trams themselves (orientation) and the light textures; the
  shuttle brick was not found (`0x515ea4` matched only a non-instance).

## Section enabling (2026-10-31)

- The race manager keeps, per player (stride `0x1bc`), three row slots of
  `0x94` bytes: previous `+0x830`, current `+0x8c4`, next `+0x958`, each
  {section, its WMOD route, row index, row pointer (`0xcf0` per row)}.
  `0x49dc90` fills them at the start (current = section 0 row 0, previous
  = the last section's last row, next = row 1 or section 1), disables all
  three (`0x4a8850`) and enables them (`0x4a8680`). `0x4a12a0` (moving on)
  disables the previous slot and enables the new next one; `0x4a09b0`
  (moving back) disables the next slot and enables the new previous one;
  both set the car's `+0x104` from the current slot (`0x4a07a0`).
- `0x4a8680` sets flag 2 on the row's kind-zero links (`+0xce8`, count
  `+0xce4`; `(link & 3) == 0`, object `0x49b0e0(link >> 2)`) whose node
  is top level (flag `0x10`, parent the world) and on their children, and
  on the 32 pointers `+0x5e4..+0x664`; `0x4a8850` disables the same nodes
  through `0x41ba90`. The walker `0x432970` returns at a disabled node
  without walking its actions or children, and the draw walk `0x432490`
  needs flag `0x1000`.
- dt_t1 has one row per section: section 1's links GDModel `0x21d`
  (WElTram action `0x21e`) and GDPath `0x21f`, section 2's the WTram
  `0x370`. Power-ups, cameras and portals are linked too (not ported).
- Native: `RaceTrack` maps each linked node to its sections and
  `CourseObjects::link` gives them to the crossing and the shuttle; an
  object runs while one of them is the player's section or a neighbour.
- **Wine, WElTram** (node `0x21d` found by its id at `+0x1c`, flags and
  frame `+0x2c` logged every 200 ms over a driven lap): flags went
  `0x10805012` → `0x10804010` on entering section 3 and back on entering
  section 0, three times. While disabled the frame stayed put; on
  re-enabling the shuttle went on from that position in the same
  direction (63 per update), and its basis never changed. So the init
  `0x4d1a90` (which would reset t to m_nOffset and re-read `+0x74` from
  the node's frame) does not run again: only the node is disabled, the
  action keeps its flags. `0x4d1c20` (the action's `+0x14`) only drops
  its collision entry (`0x404fe8`). Native: the shuttle freezes, is not
  drawn and does not hit while disabled.

## The lights' UV animation ported (2026-11-01)

**Native.** `RaceView::animate_lights`, once per frame. The model is the WTram brick's second child (GDModel `0x3b1` on dt_t1). Its batches are found at the first draw, after the power-ups have left the static world. For each vertex, from its serialized UV:
- with v < 0.5, the lamp is A when v < 0.24, else B;
- the bit comes from u: below 0.33 red, below 0.66 amber, else green;
- v becomes 0 when that lamp's bit is lit, else 0.33.

The UVs fall in two v clusters, about 0.1 (lamp A) and 0.35 (lamp B), and u around 0.2, 0.55 and higher, which fits these thresholds.

**Check.** An Xvfb capture near the crossing (`WPC_RACE_PLACE=10500,22500,0`) shows lamp A red lit on the front face. Lamp B on the side turns from red to green between two captures 4 s apart, as the cycle (1,4), (1,2), (1,1)… gives.

Ported for renderer mode 1 only (`0x6f5958`). Not compared with Wine.
