# DirectSound 3D on Windows: DirectX 7's software Pan3D, recovered (2026-11-18)

Follows `analysis/race-audio-wine-20261118.md` (Wine's dsound is not Windows') and `analysis/native-audio-20261028.md`
("Native 3D law", provisional).

## Which algorithm the game gets

The game creates its 3D buffers at `0x427ad0..0x427b88` (`IDirectSound::CreateSoundBuffer`, vtable `+0xc`):
- a 0x24-byte DirectX 7 `DSBUFFERDESC`, cleared by `rep stos` (9 dwords);
- flags 0x1f2: `DSBCAPS_STATIC | CTRL3D | CTRLFREQUENCY | CTRLPAN | CTRLVOLUME | CTRLPOSITIONNOTIFY`; the 2D
  buffers (`0x427a74`) use 0xe2;
- `guid3DAlgorithm` = `GUID_NULL` (`DS3DALG_DEFAULT`), and neither `LOCHARDWARE` nor `LOCSOFTWARE`.

So on a card with hardware 3D the card's driver did the work (not recoverable in general). Otherwise DirectX's
software 3D renders it, and for the default algorithm that is the pan-only object (the DLL's strings "Default
Software 3D Algorithm", "...substituting the Pan3D algorithm").

## The DLL

`dsound.dll` 4.07 era, 399120 bytes, dated 1999-09-09: from the game disc's own DirectX 7 redistributable
(`3Party/Directx7/Setup/directx.cab`, extracted with `cabextract` into the session scratchpad; not in the
repository). It was analysed with `rea` 6.9.0 (Ghidra 12.2 provider: `rea analyze --provider ghidra`,
`rea decompile`) and `objdump`. The image base is `0xbeaf0000`; the Pan3D object's vtable is `0xbeaf25e8`.

| Slot | Routine | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `+0x88` | `0xbeb0f923` | muted: returns `+0xd0` (set past the max distance) unless the mode is 2 (disabled) or `+0xac` is 0 |
| `+0x90` | `0xbeb0f4be` | the sound cone: inside/outside angles x pi/360, gain `+0xdc` (1 with the default 360 degree cones) |
| `+0x94` | `0xbeb0f637` | the distance gain `+0xe4` |
| `+0x98` | `0xbeb0f867` | the position update: the listener-relative vector (`0xbeb0cd3d`), then `+0xa4`, `+0x90`, `+0x94` |
| `+0xa4` | `0xbeb10299` | r = the vector's length (`0xbeb0c976`, which also gives azimuth atan2(x, z) and elevation); `+0xec` = x / r + 0.5 (0.5 when r is 0) |
| `+0xa8` | `0xbeb102fd` | applies: volume = `+0xb0` + the buffer's own volume, pan = `+0xb4`, clamped (`0xbeb0b08f`) and split into left/right (`0xbeb0b03a`) |
| `+0xb0` | `0xbeb103b5` | the 3D volume in mB |
| `+0xb4` | `0xbeb1040d` | the 3D pan in mB |

## The law

- **Distance** (`0xbeb0f637`), with d the distance (`+0xb0`), min `+0x4c`, max `+0x50`, and the listener's rolloff
  `[+0x14] + 0x5c`:
  - d >= max: d = max and the buffer is muted (`+0xd0` = 1);
  - min < d and rolloff > 0: **g = min / (min + rolloff x (d - min))**;
  - otherwise g = 1.
- **Volume** (`0xbeb103b5`), unless muted: `trunc(2000 x log2(g x cone) x 0.3010299957) - 500` mB, i.e. 20 log10 in
  dB less 5 dB.
  - The helper `0xbeb0a0a6(a, b)` is a x log2(b) (`fyl2x`); the constants are doubles at `0xbeaf2180` (2000) and
    `0xbeaf2188` (log10 2).
  - The conversion `0xbeb38d80` sets the rounding control to truncate.
  - Muted gives -10000.
- **Pan** (`0xbeb1040d`), with p = x / r + 0.5 and x the listener-right component:
  - p < 0.5: `trunc(3000 x log10(2p))`, or -10000 when p <= 0;
  - p >= 0.5: `trunc(-3000 x log10(2 - 2p))`, or +10000 when p >= 1.
  - The constants: 3000 at `0xbeaf21a8`, -log10 2 at `0xbeaf21b0`, 0.5, 1, 2.
  - So the pan is full at x / r = +-0.5, 30 degrees off the axis; elevation and front/back do not matter.
- **Split** (`0xbeb0b03a`): the volume is clamped to [-10000, 0] and the pan to +-10000. A positive pan lowers the
  left by the pan, a negative one the right.

With the game's values (rolloff 5, typical min 1000 and max 10000) the old provisional law ((min/d)^5, held at max,
no offset, +-90 degrees to +-10000) was far too steep: at d 2000 it gave -30 dB where Windows gives -15.6 - 5 =
-20.6 dB. It also panned too gently near the axis.

## Native

- `directsound3d` (`audio_mixer.cpp`) now computes this law: the gain is linear from the volume in mB (0 when muted),
  the pan is the far side's linear loss from the pan in mB.
- `RaceAudio::place` applies the game's own range fix (`0x427c50`): a negative m_MinRange is 0, and an m_MaxRange
  not above it becomes min + 0.01 (`0x510b00`).
- `native_audio_test` checks hand-worked values: -500 mB within min; -2056 at d 2000; muted past max; -2582 and full
  right at (3000, 0, 0); -1474 and full left at 45 degrees; 2929 mB at x / r 0.447. ctest 14 of 14.
- A native capture of a cc_t1 start (`WPC_AUDIO_CAPTURE`) runs with the new law. Its left/right swing is within
  about 1 dB with the music on.

## Not covered

- Hardware 3D on the card, and the HRTF modes (`dsound3d.dll`, "Full HRTF" / "Light HRTF"): the game asks for
  neither.
- Doppler (`0xbeb0fd0a`, `0xbeb0cdec`): the game's velocities were not checked, and the port has no Doppler.
- The cone: taken as the default (gain 1); the game's cone calls were not looked for.
- The mB-to-linear table (`0xbeb091e3`): taken as 10^(mB / 2000).
- No Windows capture to compare with.
