# WFire: the jet flames of TurboSpeed and FlyPower (2026-11-09)

Static reading of WPC.EXE; a native capture; **no Wine comparison**.

## The class

- **Registration** `0x481050`: string `0x52b544`, class record `0x6f28f0` (method table `0x513094`).
  The factory `0x4810a0` builds a 0x12e-byte object (base constructor `0x4041ba` → `0x41e2f0`) with
  vtable `0x5130b8`. Slots 3 (`0x481390`, the draw) and 4 (`0x481270`, the init) are its own.
- **Nodes** (general.wgm; no members of their own; flags `0x10040112`, so hidden by `0x100`): the
  car models' siblings of the body GDHModel (`[car +0x24f] +0x14`, then `+0x14` again):

  | Vehicle | Model | Body | WFire |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0 | `0x6b` | `0x3a` | `0x71`, `0x72` |
  | 2 | `0xd7` | `0x6d` | `0xdd` |
  | 5 | `0xa0` | `0x54` | `0xa9`, `0xaa` |
  | 6 | `0x86` | `0x49` | `0x8c` |

  Vehicles 1, 3 and 4 have none.
- **Init** `0x481270`: `+0x11e` = 100 (length), `+0x122` = 16 (width), `+0x126` = 64 (spread);
  the shared points `0x6f2908..0x6f297c` cleared; `+0x12a` = 0.
- **`+0x12a`** is a render-pass flag: the scene walk (`0x4249d0..0x4249fa`) sets it to 1 around the
  slot-3 call for objects of one class test (`0x40556a`), then back to 0. So a shown WFire draws in
  that pass every frame.

## The draw (`0x481390`)

With `rand()` = `0x404363` (→ `0x430ef0`), four calls per draw:

- W = rand % (`+0x126` / 16 + 1) + `+0x122`; L = rand % `+0x126` + `+0x11e` + 1.
- Ten points (`0x6f2908`, 12 bytes each): the origin; (0, W/2, 0), (W/2, 0, 0), (0, −W/2, 0),
  (−W/2, 0, 0) (`0x51026c` = 0.5, `0x510540` = −0.5); the ring (0, W), (W, 0), (0, −W), (−W, 0) at
  z = L/4 (`0x512664` = 0.25); the tip (0, 0, L).
- Colour: red 255, green (rand & 0x7f) + 0x80, blue 0, alpha rand % 80 + 0xaa.
- Eight triangles from the table `0x52b518` (4 bytes each): (0, 9, 5), (0, 9, 6), (0, 9, 7),
  (0, 9, 8), (0, 1, 5), (0, 2, 6), (0, 3, 7), (0, 4, 8). Their vertices are 0x28 bytes; only the
  first corner gets the colour, the other two are (0, 0, 0, 0).
- Renderer 1 (`0x6f5958` = 1): `0x404269` → `0x4b5550` with mode 8 and 24 vertices; renderer 2 calls
  the renderer's `+0xf0` the same way. Mode 8 has no bit `0x10`, so culling stays on, with the default
  blend and no depth write (`analysis/hit-smoke-20261107.md`).

## Native

- `RaceView::submit_fires` picks the flames from the car's running gadget (`gadget_effect` 8) or the
  FlyPower counter, builds the eight triangles in world space (the car model's frame, as for the body,
  × the local frame) and `effects` draws them after the rocket tails: no depth write, back faces culled.
- The random draws use a view-local `rand()` (214013, 2531011), not the original's shared one.
- `WPC_GADGET=0..10` (debug) gives player 1 that gadget with a full charge.
- A native capture (`cc_t1`, `--vehicle 0`, `WPC_GADGET=8`, '/' after the start): two flickering
  orange flames behind the rear exhausts, pointing back, thin with culling (one triangle of each fin
  pair faces the follow camera); drawn two-sided they look fuller. Vehicles 5 (turbo and FlyPower)
  and 0 (FlyPower) were captured over sand, where the flames show faintly.
