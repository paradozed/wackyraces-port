# Sprite orientation (`0x419b90`, 20261029)

`GD3dSprite` (vtable `0x510464`, constructor `0x4197xx`) and `WGndSprite` (vtable `0x5131c4`) share their draw, slot `+0xc` = `0x419b90`. The routine is missing from the Ghidra export; this note reads the disassembly. No Wine comparison was made.

## Fields

The sprite properties `0x09..0x10` are 8 bytes apart from `+0x122`. Constructor defaults are in brackets.

| Property | Offset | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `0x09` | `+0x122` | material id |
| `0x0a` | `+0x12a` | facing mode [0] |
| `0x0b` | `+0x132` | x anchor cx [0.5] |
| `0x0c` | `+0x13a` | y anchor cy [0.5] |
| `0x0d` | `+0x142` | size s [1]; 0 draws nothing |
| `0x0e` | `+0x14a` | aspect [0]; 0 takes the texture's height / width (`0x419c21`) and stores it |
| `0x0f` | `+0x152` | angle [0], applied by `0x40312f` after the facing |
| `0x10` | `+0x15a` | render setting [`0x80062`], copied to the quad model's `+0x12a` |

## The quad (`0x419c36`)

The vertices go into the quad model `+0x15e`, in its own xy plane (z = 0), with h = s × aspect:

| Vertex | x | y |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | −cx·s | −cy·s |
| 1 | −cx·s + s | −cy·s |
| 2 | −cx·s | −cy·s + h |
| 3 | −cx·s + s | −cy·s + h |

**The y anchor scales by s, not by h.** The bounds go to `+0x5c..+0x6c`, and the radius × `0x51026c` to `+0x58`.

## The facing (`0x419d37`, mode `+0x12a`)

- **1.** The sprite's frame (the argument), unchanged.
- **2.** The camera frame (the renderer `+0x1838`, `+4`), inverted (`0x403c74`) and composed with the sprite's frame, so the quad lies in the camera's axes. Nothing is drawn when the sprite is behind the camera (`0x419db8`).
- **Any other value.**
  - The camera's position C and forward axis come from its frame (`0x4038f5`, `0x4024fa`), and the sprite's position P from its own.
  - Nothing is drawn when (P − C) · forward < 0.
  - Otherwise n = (P − C) horizontal, normalized, and `0x4050d8` builds the rows:
    - X = (n.z, 0, −n.x);
    - Y = (0, 1, 0);
    - Z = (n.x, 0, n.z);
    - translation = P.
  - So the quad turns about the **world** up axis to face the camera position. The frame's own rotation and scale are dropped.

The angle `0x0f` then rotates the matrix (`0x40312f`). The quad model takes the sprite's `+0x68` and object flag `0x20` (`0x4200e0`: the world frame from `0x405204` instead of the per-section table `0x5472d0`), and goes to the renderer's model queue (slot `+0x40`).

## Corpus

Every `GD3dSprite` and `WGndSprite` in `cc_t1`, `sh_t1`, `dt_t1`, `ct_t1` and `general.wgm` has mode 0 and angle 0. cy ranges from −0.32 to 1.19, and aspects from 0.3 to 4.

## Native

- `game_world` builds both classes' quads with the recovered y anchor. `facing` is set from `0x0a`; mode 1 keeps the transformed quad. Mode 1 sprites were previously skipped as unsupported.
- `face_billboards(batches, camera)` turns the quads each frame: mode 2 by the camera's right and up axes, the others about (0, 1, 0) toward the camera position.
- **Changes on screen:**
  - `cc_t1`/`dt_t1`'s `WGndSprite`s (cacti, signs) now turn too; they were fixed to their serialized plane.
  - `GD3dSprite`s face the camera position rather than lying parallel to the screen, and keep the world vertical rather than the frame's up axis.
  - Sprites with cy ≠ 0 and aspect ≠ 1 sit at the recovered height.
- **Checked:** in screenshots of the `ct_t1` and `cc_t1` starts (headless, in the scratchpad), pines and cacti stand upright, face-on. The behind-the-camera cull is left to clipping.
- **Not ported:** nonzero angles (they are still rejected).

## Colour and fog (open)

- The quad is a `GDModel` drawn with the sprite's material. The routine writes no colours.
- `0x4b1c32` turns Direct3D lighting off (`SetRenderState(0x89, 0)`, after clipping `0x88` = 1), so vertex colours come from the engine.
- The sprite materials have saturated diffuse colours (on `ct_t1`, (0.19, 0.56, 0.38), (0, 0.435, 0.19), (0.49, 0.61, 0.98), …). Natively, textured materials draw at colour 1. Whether the engine modulates by the diffuse colour, and how fog applies, needs the renderer's vertex colour path or a Wine capture of `ct_t1`. The paler pines are not fixed.

### Update 2026-11-01: closed

A Wine capture of `ct_t1`'s start (same camera, Xvfb, 1024×768) shows the pines at the same colours as the native build: foliage samples differ by at most about 8 %, which the slightly different camera height explains. Sprites draw at colour 1; the material colours are not used. The paler impression came from the course meshes. See `analysis/vertex-colours-20261101.md`.
