# sh_t3's log ride (WLogRide), 2026-11-02

## Data

On `sh_t3`, WLogRide #124 has an UVScroll action and these children:
- GDPathQ #126, with 18 points in property `0x7c` (GDPath's layout);
- nine WLogBase models (GMDL `0x123`).

Its members:
- m_length `0x24c` = 500 (`+0x88`);
- m_width `0x24d` = 188 (`+0x90`);
- m_dist `0x24e` = 5000 (`+0x98`);
- m_vel `0x24f` = 50 (`+0xa0`).

Each WLogBase has one PRIM of sensor group 2 whose shape the native parser does not decode (radius 0). It is presumably the model mesh as ground.

WLogJam #615 holds six WLogModels, each with a cylinder PRIM (radius 100, height 500, flags `0x40000`), and five GDPaths.

## Update `0x4cc450`

1. While `+0x23a` (2 from the init) is non-zero, decrement it and return.
2. Add m_vel to `+0x246`. If it passes (m_length + 50) × 2 + m_dist (`0x512a5c` = 50), subtract that.
3. For each WLogBase from `+0x242` along `+0x14`:
   - `0x4be7f0(path, m_length, &d, &segment, frame, 0xe)` builds the frame at the midpoint of the points at d and d + m_length;
   - on success, `0x43b4a0(pi)` and the frame is written;
   - the log's motion is kept in `0x2aaf9c8[i]` when every axis is below 2 × m_vel;
   - d then grows by m_length + 50, or by m_dist after every third log.
4. Every car of the object list 7 whose `+0x89f` (the last ground object) is one of these WLogBase is moved by that log's motion. `0x468620(1)` then refreshes its frame. `+0x89f` is cleared.

## Native

- `CourseObjects` loads `LogRide` (points in world space; the logs' frames start at their serialized frames) and runs it, with the same row enabling as the other objects.
- `RaceView` takes the logs out of the static world and draws them at their frames.
- `native_drive_test sh_t3.WGM` checks the nine logs, the idle updates, the offset, the in-group gap (543) and a log's motion.

## Provisional and not ported

- The frame's axes: flags `0xe` choose the chord axis. Natively z runs along the chord, x is horizontal, and the pi turn is applied about y. This is not checked under Wine.
- Cars standing on and carried by logs: this needs the logs' ground (the sensor-group-2 PRIM, presumably the mesh) in the ground queries and `+0x89f`. The native ground is static WGNR.
- WLogJam (`0x4c4940`) and WLogModel (`0x4c4490`): six logs rolling along their GDPaths with m_gravity 3, m_rugozas 0.8, m_surlodas 1 and m_maxrot 0.26, and their cylinder hits.
- The UVScroll action.
- Not compared with Wine.
